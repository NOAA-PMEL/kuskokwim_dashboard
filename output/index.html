Welcome

    This ocean temperature dashboard is a collaborative effort to make information about real-time and predicted Bering Sea temperatures more accessible.
    Jump in and begin using the dashboard by clicking on your region of interest.
    Or if you would like to learn more about the context and methods supporting the dashboard, please go to the "About this Project" tab.
    Our team includes the University of Washington - CICOES, NOAA Pacific Marine Environmental Laboratory, and the Alaska Seafood Cooperative, with addtional funding from the North Pacific Research Board
