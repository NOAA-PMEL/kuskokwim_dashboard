Kuskokwim Bay Region Ocean Temperature

How to use this Site

  • Main plot to the left is zoomable and clickable.
  • Layers can be turned on and off via the menu in the top right of the map.
  • Clicking an ADF&G Statistical Area (grid boxes near Kuskokwim Bay) will pop up a chart showing temperature predictions.
  • Clicking the pop-up will open a new full-page view of temperature predictions for that Statistical Area.

          

        


        

      

      

        
        

          

            
Further Details:

            
Our team used two seasons of field data collection combined with a range of other data sources to generate predictions of temperature by ADF&G Statistical Area. We invite you to explore predictions using the maps presented here. To learn more, visit About this Project.
